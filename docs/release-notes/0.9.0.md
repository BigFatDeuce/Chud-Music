**Faster and smoother, especially on modest PCs and network libraries**

Measured with a library of 20 000 files:

| | 0.8.0 | 0.9.0 |
|---|---|---|
| First scan | 32 s | 10 s |
| Longest freeze of the app during the scan | 8.3 s | 0.17 s |
| Rescan with nothing changed | 2.4 s | 0.9 s |
| Memory | 1 004 MB | 894 MB |

- **Library scan:** several files are read at once (much faster on a network share), and songs are no longer read entirely just to learn their duration.
- **No more needless reloads:** playing a song, scanning or looking for covers no longer reloads your whole library each time.
- **Small covers everywhere:** lists, queue, Home, player bar and playlist mosaics use small copies of the covers, made in the background.
- **Network folders** (NAS, shared drives, network drives) are detected and checked for changes every hour instead of being watched continuously, which was slow or did not work on a network share. Scan the library by hand (Library › … › Scan the library) to see changes immediately.
- **Modest PCs** (4 cores or fewer, or less than 6 GB of memory) start with reduced effects. You can change this in Settings, and your choice is kept.
- Lighter drawing of the ambient colours behind the window and of the spectrum.

**Translations**
"Synced Lyrics", "Start mix (Online)" and the names of the video clip effects are now translated in every language.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
