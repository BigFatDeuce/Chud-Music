**Chud Music 1.0**

The first stable version: your files, YouTube Music, radio from all over the world and synced lyrics, in one fast and light player for Windows and Linux.

**Prefer your own files in playlists**
Playlist › … › *Replace with my local files…* looks for the YouTube Music tracks of the playlist that are also in your library: same song, same artist, same length. You see the list first; once you confirm, the playlist plays your files instead, offline and in full quality, at the same place in the list. Live, remix, cover or acoustic versions are never taken for the original.

**Remove a wrong cover**
Right-click a track (or a selection of tracks) › *Remove the found cover* removes a cover that Chud Music found online and got wrong. It is not looked up again for these tracks. Your music files are never touched.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
