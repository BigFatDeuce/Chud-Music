**Chud Music 1.3.0**

Hide YouTube Music artists you never want to hear, pause the scanning of a library folder, delete every playlist in one go, and a clear answer when YouTube asks to "confirm you're not a bot".

**YouTube Music**
- *Hide artist*: right-click a YouTube Music track or an artist card (or use the button on the artist's page). The artist no longer shows in searches (songs, albums, artists) or in mixes, featured appearances included. Undo right away from the notification, or later in *Settings › YouTube Music › Hidden artists*: only their number is shown there, and the list opens when you ask for it.
- When YouTube shows "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" in its player (it depends on the connection: a VPN, a lot of plays in a row…), Chud Music now tries the video once more, then explains what happened and moves on to the next track within seconds, instead of a silent player for 25 s. The track is no longer marked unavailable. Chud Music still never signs in to a Google account; the check usually clears by itself after a few minutes.

**Library**
- *Settings › Library*: each folder has its own *Scan / Pause* switch. A paused folder is not read at all (not at startup, not on changes, not by *Rescan*), and its tracks stay in the library as they are. Switched back on, it is scanned at once.
- *Recently played* now holds the last 20 tracks, on Home and in the Library.

**Playlists**
- *Settings › Playlists › Delete all playlists*, asked twice (the second time: it cannot be undone). Your music files and the library are never touched; *Export all* is suggested first.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
