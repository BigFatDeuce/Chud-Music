**Rewind the radio**
The last 30 minutes of the station you are listening to stay in memory. The seek bar now covers them: go back to hear what you missed, then return to live with the "Live" button. The delay is shown next to the bar, and pausing a station no longer loses anything — it goes on from where you paused.

**Record the radio**
A record button sits right of the visualizer while a radio plays. It records what you hear, as the station broadcasts it (MP3, AAC…, no re-encoding), into your Music folder by default — change it in Settings › Radio.

**Video clip effects**
One effect at a time: choosing another one replaces the first.

**Fixes**
In a narrow window, the YouTube video no longer covers the menu: when the menu opens, the video moves into it, or beside it when there is not enough room.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
