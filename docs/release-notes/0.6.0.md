**Albums with the same name**
Two albums with the same name (an original and a remaster, two rips…) are no longer mixed up: their folder tells them apart, so the next track always stays in the album you are playing. A "CD1" / "Disc 2" folder still counts as one album.

**Search in playlists**
Playlists get a search box, like the Library (title, artist, album; accents and case ignored).

**Clickable artists and albums**
Artist and album names of your files open the Library on that artist or album, from the Library, playlists and the player.

**The list follows the music**
When the playing track changes and is out of view, the open list scrolls back to it — handy in shuffle mode in a big playlist.

**Small albums hidden**
Library › Albums hides albums of fewer than 4 tracks by default, so singles and stray tracks do not clutter the grid. One click shows them; turn it off in Settings › Library.

**Home**
A refresh button on "Random" draws another selection. Playlist thumbnails now show random covers from the playlist.

**Video clip effects**
Five picture effects in the video clip tab: VHS, Glitch, CRT, Noir and Neon, combinable and remembered. They apply to covers and pictures; the YouTube player is never altered, so they are disabled on YouTube videos.

**Player bar**
Clicking the clip, visualizer or lyrics button again while its tab is shown closes the tab.

**Fixes**
The mini player shows the video again when a YouTube clip was playing in the video clip tab.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
