**Enlarged player bar (YouTube player docked)**
- Next to the lyrics, a column now shows the artist, the album, your listening history and the links to learn more (YouTube Music, Wikipedia, Grokipedia, Last.fm, Discogs).
- In small windows (reduced 16:9, portrait), the lyrics or the track card now show next to the player, with the links.
- Fix: when the track changed, the previous lyrics could stay on screen over the new track card.

**Spectrum moving with YouTube**
The little spectrum in the player bar now follows YouTube's music too. It measures the sound levels of the official player; playback is not changed and nothing is recorded.

**Signal skin**
On the home page, a retro dither lens follows the mouse over the whole "Now playing" box, with a phosphor trail. The buttons stay clickable.
