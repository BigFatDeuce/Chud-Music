**Playback speed**
A small speed chip sits right above the remaining time in the player bar: 0.5× to 2×, for your files and for YouTube Music alike, with the pitch kept. Right-click it to go back to normal speed. The speed is remembered.

**Video clip button**
A new button left of "Shuffle" opens the video clip tab of what is playing, for your files and for YouTube Music tracks.

**Start mix (Online) for your files**
Right-click one of your tracks › "Start mix (Online)": Chud Music finds the same song on YouTube Music and starts its mix.

**No subtitles on YouTube videos**
Subtitles are always off in the YouTube player, even when a video turns them on by itself.

**Signal skin everywhere**
The glitchy buttons of the Signal skin now apply to every page: play, shuffle and menu buttons in the Library, playlists, albums, YouTube Music, and the play buttons on album covers.

**Settings**
The ad blocker is now always the last setting of the YouTube Music box, with its statistics right under it.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
