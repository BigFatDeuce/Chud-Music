**Chud Music 1.2.0**

Covers are found faster and more reliably, the video clip tab fits every screen and stays full screen across tracks, duplicates keep the best copy.

**Missing covers**
- *Find missing covers* now asks Deezer, then iTunes, instead of YouTube Music: faster, steadier, and no longer refused after a few hundred albums. Same result as before: the album cover, else the song's, else a picture of the artist, else "?". Pictures still go to Chud Music's own folder only; your files are never touched.
- If a service answers "too many requests", the search goes on with the other one, and an album nobody could be asked about is not marked "?": it is looked for again next time.

**Video clip tab**
- *Large* and *Beside* now always show the whole 16:9 picture, centred, as big as the window allows. On displays scaled to 125 % or 150 %, *Large* no longer runs past the bottom of the page.
- Full screen stays full screen when the music changes kind: a YouTube video followed by one of your files shows its cover (before, an empty full screen that still wanted Esc), and a file followed by a YouTube video shows the video (before, a black screen). Esc leaves, and a double click on a cover toggles it.

**YouTube Music**
- While the player is small (sidebar, player bar), YouTube is asked for 480p at most: sharper is not visible at that size and saves bandwidth. On the clip tab and in full screen, YouTube chooses the quality itself.
- The small spectrum in the player bar now always moves while a YouTube track plays, including on computers where the sound level of YouTube's player cannot be measured.

**Playlists**
- *Find duplicates…* keeps the best copy instead of the first one: a lossless file (FLAC, ALAC, WAV…) rather than an MP3, the MP3 with the higher bit rate rather than the lower one, one of your files rather than its YouTube Music twin. With the same quality, the first one stays.
- A video file (mp4, webm…) is never treated as the duplicate of a song with the same title. Videos are only compared with each other, and the better one stays.

**Settings**
- About: a short presentation of Chud Music More, the Android app of the family, with a link to its page.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
