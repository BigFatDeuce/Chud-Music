**Paste a YouTube or Spotify link in YouTube Music**
Paste a link to a track, album or playlist into the YouTube Music search box: Chud Music finds the same music on YouTube Music, ready to play or to save as a playlist. YouTube links open as they are. Spotify tracks are looked up one by one: in our tests, 16 of 18 tracks of an album and 48 of 50 of a playlist were found, in a few seconds. Tracks that could not be found are listed apart, and you can search for them with a click. Spotify artist links open the artist's YouTube Music page. Only the first 100 tracks of a Spotify playlist can be read.

**Clickable artists and albums**
On YouTube Music tracks, the artist and album names open their pages in Chud Music: in lists, in the player bar and on the track card. You can turn this off in Settings › YouTube Music.

**Find a local artist on YouTube Music**
Right-click a track from your files › "Find the artist on YouTube Music" opens that artist's YouTube Music page.

**Tabs can be moved**
Drag a tab left or right to reorder it (or Ctrl+Shift+Page Up / Page Down). Home and Library stay first. The order is kept after a restart.

**Signal skin: a glitch clock**
The Home clock became a broadcast timecode: ticking seconds, a blinking REC light, digits that scramble before they settle, a red/cyan signal tear every few seconds, a slow roll bar, and a date that types itself in (Effects: Full).
