**Video clip tab**
Right-click a track › "Open the video clip": the music plays in its own tab, big, with its lyrics on the right (synced lyrics follow the music; click a line to jump to it).
- YouTube Music songs play as their official music video when one is found (same song, same title and cover).
- Local files show their cover. Without a cover, the artist's picture from YouTube Music. Without that, a looping VHS tape that plays and pauses with the music.
- "Large" makes the picture bigger and moves the lyrics below it, to scroll at your own pace. "Full screen" fills the screen (Esc to leave).
- The next tracks open in the same layout.

**Lyrics button**
When a local file has lyrics, a "Synced Lyrics" or "Lyrics" button appears next to its badges in the player bar. It opens the Lyrics page.

**Right-click the playing cover**
On the cover in the player bar or in the sidebar: open the video clip, find the artist on YouTube Music, or show the file in its folder.

**Visualizer button**
A new button right after "Repeat" in the player bar opens the visualizer.

**Home: Random**
A "Random" shelf between "Recently played" and "Recently added": tracks from your library, drawn at each launch.

**Signal skin**
- New player-bar buttons: a square play button with viewfinder corners that breathes while playing, a red/cyan split and a short loss of sync when you hover a button, and a segmented volume meter.
- The scanlines over the window and the covers are gone. The playing cover now glitches for a few frames every few seconds instead (Effects: Full).
