**Chud Music 1.4.0**

Gapless playback, crossfade, volume normalisation, a sleep timer, smart playlists, your own playlist covers, and a much sturdier library (hundreds of thousands of tracks, several NAS).

**Playback** (local files)
- *Gapless*: tracks follow each other without any silence, for live and classical albums. On by default (*Settings › Playback*). MP3, AAC and Opus keep whatever their encoder left at the edges.
- *Crossfade*: 1 to 12 s, off by default. Never between two consecutive tracks of an album.
- *Volume normalisation (ReplayGain)*: by track or by album, from the file's tags, or measured once by Chud Music. Your files are never modified and the volume never clips.
- *Sleep timer* (moon button): after a duration, at the end of the track, or at the end of the album.
- *Queue*: drag to reorder, select and remove several tracks, clear, save as a playlist.
- *Resume*: the last track, its position and the queue come back at the next start, paused.

**Playlists**
- *Smart playlists*: genre, artist, album, title, folder, year, plays, date added, last played, duration; any number of rules, exclusions, order, maximum. Ready-made: Never played, Rediscover, Recently added, Most played. They update by themselves and cover the local library.
- *Covers*: right-click a playlist (sidebar, Playlists page, or the cover of the open playlist) to choose its image, or go back to the automatic one.
- *Recently played* keeps the last 10 tracks, with *Clear all*. History and statistics are kept.

**Library and network**
- Very large libraries: the interface loads them in pages, then only what changed. Tested with 200,000 tracks.
- Several network shares at once: a slow or absent one no longer holds back the others. A share that comes back is found by itself, and its unchanged files are not read again.
- Fixed: a folder that could not be read while a share dropped made its tracks leave the library. They now stay.
- If a share stops answering, playback waits for it, says why, and resumes. Error messages now say what to do.
- Files opened in quick succession give a single *Play* offer instead of several.

SMB and WebDAV shares work through the system (network path, mapped drive, mount); Chud Music has no WebDAV client of its own.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
