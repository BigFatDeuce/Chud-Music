**Chud Music 1.0.1**

A reliability update: playback, radio and updates behave better when things are slow or go wrong.

**Playback**
- The track you pick is no longer skipped when the previous one ends while yours is still being prepared (ALAC, WMA, AIFF and other formats converted on first play).
- Play/pause while a track is loading now cancels it: nothing starts behind your back, and the track stays there, ready to play. Before, the button did nothing until the track had loaded (up to 25 seconds for YouTube Music). The button shows it: pause icon, *Cancel loading*.

**Radio**
- A station that does not answer gives one notification, in your language, instead of two (one of them a raw technical message), and the next station of the list is tried.

**Updates**
- An update that was found stays offered when a later check cannot reach GitHub, instead of being replaced by an error until the next check.
- The *Update* button of a notification no longer starts a second download when one is running or finished.

`ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz`: the exact source code of the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Not needed to use Chud Music.
