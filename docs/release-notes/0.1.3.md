**Links always next to the lyrics**
With Windows display scaling above 100 % (125 %, 150 %…), in a window that is not maximized, or on a smaller screen, the links to learn more about the artist moved under the lyrics. They now always stay on the right: when space is short, their column gets narrower and the links wrap onto several lines.

**Playlists from the "+" of the tabs**
The "+" next to the tabs (and Ctrl+O) now offers "Playlists" first, before "Files…" and "A folder…".

**Reorder "Opened files"**
Tracks in the "Opened files" tab can now be moved up or down: right-click › Move up / Move down, Alt+↑ / Alt+↓, or drag and drop within the tab. The track playing is not interrupted, playback follows the new order, and the order is kept after a restart.
