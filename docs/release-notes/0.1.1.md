**New: lyrics and track card in the player bar**

When the YouTube player moves into the bottom bar (narrow window, playlists shown, or not enough height):
- the buttons, the seek bar and the volume stay exactly where they usually are;
- above them, the **synced lyrics** scroll line by line (click a line to jump there); without timing, the text scrolls along with the song;
- without lyrics, the **track card** takes their place: artist and album, your listening history, and links to learn more about the artist (YouTube Music, Wikipedia, Grokipedia, Last.fm, Discogs).
